Prism Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Prism is an experimental mobile application and Web3 project focused on network visibility, validators, wallets, useful work, and participation. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Prism mobile application.

1. Information Prism may process

Prism is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • public blockchain or network information, such as wallet addresses, balances, validator data, participation metrics, and other publicly available protocol data;
  • technical information required to operate the app, diagnose errors, or communicate with backend services;
  • purchase and subscription status when you use optional Prism Pro features.

Prism does not require you to provide sensitive personal information merely to view public network data.

2. Purchases and Prism Pro

Optional Prism Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. These services may process purchase-related identifiers, transaction status, device or app information, and subscription entitlement data as necessary to provide and manage purchases.

Payment card details are handled by Google Play and are not received or stored directly by Prism.

3. Advertising

Prism does not display third-party advertising and does not use user information to serve personalized ads.

4. How information is used

Information processed by Prism is used only for purposes such as:

  • displaying Prism network, wallet, validator, and participation information;
  • providing app functionality and optional Prism Pro features;
  • maintaining security, reliability, and troubleshooting;
  • improving the user experience and protocol tooling.

5. Data sharing

Prism does not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by service providers strictly where needed to operate app functionality, including Google Play and RevenueCat for purchases and subscriptions.

6. Public blockchain and network data

Blockchain addresses, transactions, validator records, participation records, and similar protocol information may be public by design. Information written to a public or distributed ledger may be visible to others and may not be removable by Prism.

7. Data retention

Prism aims to retain only the information necessary to provide the relevant feature or comply with technical and legal obligations. Data controlled by third-party providers is subject to their own retention practices and privacy policies.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application data and communications. However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Prism is not directed to children under 13. Prism does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Your choices and requests

If you believe Prism holds information about you that should be corrected or deleted, or if you have a privacy question, you can contact the project through the Prism GitHub repository. Requests concerning information held directly by Google Play or RevenueCat may also need to be submitted to those providers.

11. Third-party services

Prism may rely on third-party services whose own privacy terms apply, including:

  • Google Play / Google Play Billing
  • RevenueCat

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Prism evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page by updating the effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Prism project through its public GitHub repository:
